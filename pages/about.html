About Y.G. Trading

A Markham, Ontario manufacturer, importer and wholesale distributor of packaging, food-processing and store-supply consumables.

From our warehouse in Markham we supply grocery retailers, food processors, food-service operators and wholesale distributors across the Greater Toronto Area and beyond.

Our range covers the everyday essentials of a fresh-food operation: PET and PP trays, PVC cling film, absorbent pads, produce and shopping bags, take-out containers, labels, tapes, gloves and janitorial supplies. Most of these lines are standardized and kept in stock for quick turnaround. Where a customer needs something specific, we produce to specification through our own overseas production lines and factory partners of decades' standing.

We work as a single, accountable supplier: manufacturer for custom and private-label programs, importer with container-direct supply, and local wholesaler holding stock in Markham for fast delivery. Alongside the standard lines we carry our own brands, E-Pack and Vanguard.
